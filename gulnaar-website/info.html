    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I track my order?
    Use the Track Order page with your Order ID + mobile number. After dispatch you'll also get the TCS CN for live tracking.
    How does Cash on Delivery work?
    Choose COD at checkout. We call to confirm, then ship via TCS. Keep the exact amount ready for the rider. Maximum Rs 50,000 per COD order.
    I paid via JazzCash/Easypaisa — when is my order confirmed?
    Your order sits in "Awaiting Payment Verification" until our team checks your transaction ID in the wallet app — usually within a few hours (9am–9pm). You'll get an SMS once verified.
    Can I change my address after ordering?
    Yes, if the order isn't packed yet. Call or WhatsApp us immediately with your Order ID.
    Do you deliver outside Pakistan?
    Not yet — nationwide Pakistan only for now.
